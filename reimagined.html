About The Project

These songs already existed.
They were already on the charts.
Now they sound like what they always wanted to be.

NOAH Reimagined takes seven of the strongest originals from a catalog spanning 2012–2024 and rebuilds them with contemporary production. Not remixes. Not covers. The same compositions — the same melodies, the same lyrics, the same authorship — reimagined using Sunolicious, a production framework built around AI-assisted arrangement and sound design.

The source material is registered with the US Copyright Office, documented with ASCAP, and chart-proven across Billboard, UK Music Week, and DJ Times. The rights are clean. The catalog is real. The tools evolved — the authorship didn't.
